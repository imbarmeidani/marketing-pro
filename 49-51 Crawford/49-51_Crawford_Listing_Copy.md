# 49-51 Crawford Street — Listing Copy

Source copy for the whole campaign. The **Core MLS listing** is the foundation; the two
audience angles and the plan's captions/ads should all trace back to it. Voice: warm coach,
plain-spoken, optimistic, never salesy. Sentence case. No emoji. At most one exclamation point.

> **Compliance:** Keep rent/return **projections OUT of MLS public remarks** (many MLSs
> restrict them) — they live in Agent Remarks, the flyers, and audience ads, always labeled
> "estimated/projected, not guaranteed." Fair-housing rule: describe the **home**, not the buyer.
